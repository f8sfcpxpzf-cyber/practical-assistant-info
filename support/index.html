Practical Assistant Beta Support

The current Friends & Family beta is for United States external testers on iPhone and iPad through TestFlight. Android distribution is being prepared separately. This support page does not itself enroll you in the beta or provide a TestFlight download link.

For help, bug reports or privacy questions, email rbdebord@gmail.com. Practical Assistant is developed and supported by Robert DeBord. Support is handled personally; an immediate response is not guaranteed.

What to test

Tell us whether the Payday Report is clear, common actions are easy to find, setup and help make sense, and any workflow takes too many taps. Wording, layout and usability feedback are welcome. Review captured and spoken values before saving. The app is a planning aid: verify important amounts and due dates against your own records. It does not move money or pay bills for you.

Send a useful bug report

Use the subject Practical Assistant beta — [short issue description] and include:

  • App version and build, such as 1.0.0 (2).
  • Device model and iOS/iPadOS version; Android version when relevant.
  • What you were trying to do and the steps that led to the issue.
  • What you expected, what happened and whether it repeats.
  • Approximate time and timezone for notification problems, plus whether Focus was active.
  • A redacted screenshot or small made-up example, only if useful.

You can also send feedback through TestFlight. Its feedback and diagnostics are shared with Apple and the developer. Do not send passwords, app/device PINs, bank credentials, full account numbers, unredacted financial documents or private Calendar details. Review screenshots for sensitive information before sending. Do not send a complete device log or database unless a specific safe collection method has been agreed.

Report these promptly

For missing records, incorrect financial results, unintended record changes, private information exposed unexpectedly, or crashes preventing use, put URGENT BETA ISSUE in the subject. Stop relying on the affected result, preserve the current installation and describe what happened. Do not keep editing affected records merely to make the result look correct. Cosmetic problems and suggestions can be sent as ordinary feedback.

Protect your data during troubleshooting

Keep independent copies of your original financial records. The beta encrypts planner data on the device and has no tested recovery after deletion or loss of the original device key. Reinstalling, clearing app data or moving to a new device is not a recovery method. Some iOS Keychain items may remain after deletion, but they cannot recreate deleted records.

If the app reports that stored data is unavailable, unlock the device first if it has just restarted and use the app’s Retry action. If the problem continues, stop and contact support. Do not uninstall, clear data, reset security, delete keys or replace the installation to troubleshoot. Update the existing app through the approved beta channel without deleting it first.

Known beta limitations

  • Time Sensitive notification behavior under the actual distribution build remains a documented beta observation item. Notifications may be delayed, suppressed, or allowed to interrupt certain Focus modes depending on operating-system settings. Do not rely on the beta as your only payment reminder.
  • Financial notification amounts are hidden by default. Enabling them may expose information through previews or connected devices according to your settings.
  • Final iPad notification presentation and tap routing were not directly witnessed during acceptance; the beta proceeds with that recorded limitation and supporting automated/shared-platform evidence.
  • Camera recognition and on-device speech availability depend on the device, OS and language. Review results before saving; use manual entry if capture is unavailable or inaccurate.
  • The interface is currently English. Multi-currency support does not mean that every locale’s numeric/date-entry convention or currency display format is supported.
  • Older supported OS/device combinations have not all been physically tested.
  • TestFlight builds expire. Keep the app installed, watch for approved updates and contact support if a build expires; do not delete your data to resolve expiration.

Permissions and privacy

Optional Calendar, camera, microphone/speech and notification permissions can be managed in device settings. Declining them affects the associated feature. Do not change unrelated device settings to troubleshoot. Contact support before making changes that could affect your records or device security.

Read the Privacy Policy for local storage, diagnostics, Android SDK disclosures, backups and data choices. Never provide Apple/Google account credentials to support.
