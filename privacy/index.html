Practical Assistant Privacy Policy

Policy version: 1.0 — Friends & Family beta

Practical Assistant is developed and supported by Robert DeBord. This policy describes the app and its Privacy and Support pages. The initial external beta is limited to United States testers using TestFlight on iPhone or iPad. Android-specific information below applies to the Android build when made available; it does not describe an SDK used by the Apple build.

Privacy questions: rbdebord@gmail.com.

Your planner records

Practical Assistant processes the information you enter or choose to import, including income, bills, expenses, reminders, account references, saved currency conversions, Calendar-related plans and preferences. These records support planning and reports on your device. The app does not operate a cloud financial-record account, synchronize your planner database to our servers, connect to your bank or make payments. You do not need to provide bank credentials.

Local storage does not mean that no information ever leaves your device. Optional online services, platform diagnostics and feedback are described below.

Optional access and local processing

  • Calendar: With permission, the app uses events from your selected calendars to suggest upcoming expenses. The production app does not create or modify native Calendar events. Your calendar provider, including iCloud, may separately synchronize its own calendars. Revoking permission does not erase previously accepted planned expenses or saved planning history.
  • Camera and files: Images and PDFs you choose are processed for information you review before saving. Recognition runs locally using Apple Vision on iPhone/iPad and Google ML Kit on Android. The recognition path does not upload the document image or extracted text for remote recognition. Temporary working copies use the device’s app sandbox protection and are cleaned up after processing or on a later launch following interruption; your original documents are not deleted by that cleanup.
  • Microphone and speech: Voice capture uses supported on-device speech recognition. If that capability is unavailable, the app does not switch to cloud speech recognition. Review recognized entries before saving.
  • App Lock: Optional PIN and biometric authentication control access to the app. Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint matching are handled by the operating system; the app does not receive biometric templates. App Lock is separate from financial-storage encryption.
  • Notifications: The operating system receives scheduled notification content. Financial amounts are hidden by default and can be enabled explicitly. Device previews, notification history, connected devices and Focus settings also affect what is shown. Later app cleanup cannot remove screenshots or guarantee removal of every external copy.

You can decline optional permissions or revoke them in your device’s settings. The associated feature may then be unavailable; manual planner entry remains available.

Online services and diagnostic information

Exchange rates: Online rate lookups contact Frankfurter over HTTPS with currency codes, dates and any selected rate-provider filters. The app does not include your financial amounts, account references or planner records in these requests. The service receives network connection information needed to handle the request. See Frankfurter’s service information.

Apple TestFlight: Apple collects beta testing information including crash logs, usage information, device/app information and feedback submitted through TestFlight, and makes applicable beta-testing information available to the developer. If you join through a public TestFlight link, your name and email address are not shown to us and you normally appear as an anonymous tester. If you voluntarily provide an email address with feedback, that address may be shown with that feedback. Apple can associate TestFlight activity with your Apple Account under Apple’s own privacy practices. We use information made available to us through TestFlight to investigate problems and improve Practical Assistant. You may stop participating in the beta through TestFlight at any time. See TestFlight & Privacy and Apple’s tester-information guidance.

Android ML Kit: The Android build uses Google’s unbundled ML Kit text-recognition SDK, with its model supplied through Google Play services. Google documents that this SDK collects device/app information, device or installation identifiers, performance information, configuration, input/output sizes, feature events and errors for diagnostics and usage analytics. Model downloads may require a connection. This is separate from uploading your document for recognition. See Google’s ML Kit disclosure.

Support and sharing: If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send; email providers also process that correspondence. The app’s feedback draft can include version/build, platform, OS and model information. It does not automatically attach financial records or send the message. If you choose to export/share a report or open an external website, the recipient or service handles what you send under its own policy. We do not use planner records for advertising or sell them. The app has no advertising or separate product-analytics backend; that does not eliminate the platform and SDK diagnostics described above.

Encryption, backups and recovery

Saved financial records use AES-GCM encryption with device-bound key protection through Apple Keychain or Android Keystore mechanisms. Keys are not hard-coded into the app. Encryption is not a guarantee against every device compromise or information you choose to display or share.

The iOS financial snapshot and legacy preference storage are excluded from supported backup paths, and their keys are device-only. Android app backup/device-transfer exclusions protect its local storage. These controls do not erase older backups, original documents, native calendars, exports or unrelated operating-system data.

This beta has no tested recovery after app-data deletion, loss of the original device key or transfer to another device. We cannot reconstruct your local financial database or provide a key/PIN recovery service. Keep independent financial records. Do not uninstall, clear app data or reset the app as a troubleshooting step without contacting support first.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Saved planner data remains on your device for your use, subject to the app’s editing/deletion controls and any retained financial history. Removing permissions alone does not delete saved records. Removing app data can permanently destroy it. Some iOS Keychain items may remain after app deletion; those items alone cannot recover deleted records.

We retain support correspondence and diagnostic information we receive only as needed to respond, investigate issues, maintain security and meet applicable obligations. Email us to request access to or deletion of information you sent us. We cannot remotely inspect or delete your device-local planner database. Apple, Google, email providers and hosting providers control their own copies and retention; deleting our copy does not delete theirs.

These web pages and policy updates

These pages use GitHub Pages hosting. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels or contact forms. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes; see GitHub Pages information and the GitHub Privacy Statement. Email contact uses your chosen email service.

We will update this policy and its version when the app’s practices change. Any later organization identity or public-release practices will be described here before applying them to that release.
